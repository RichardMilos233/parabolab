# D48: sparse Fourier averaging of actual derivative fields

Date: 2026-09-29. Conventional theorem accepted after the complete frozen
R23 derivation and independent T100 audit. This changes the sample law;
it is not a variance improvement or an amendment to frozen E5.

## Exact scope

Retain D43/D44 on the normalized unit d-torus: fixed d>=1, kappa,tau>0,
fixed inward polynomial reaction, continuous known g with ||g||inf<1
and evaluation cost G, continuous unknown v, direction e=v-g, and fixed
derivative order j>=1. Only scalar v(x) acquisitions reveal the input,
including repeats. A Taylor use separately requires its whole segment
inside the open unit ball. The paid model has ideal real arithmetic,
elementary functions, random primitives, floor and finite index words;
this is not a bounded-word or bit-complexity statement.

For integers N>=0 and M>=1, put r=d+1, R=r+d=2d+1 and K=(2N+1)^d.
There is an actual random real Fourier array with mean
P_N D^j S_tau(g)[e,...,e], obtained from M independent sparse trials,
such that

    queries <= j M on every seed,
    E work <= C [M(1+G)+K],
    E ||output-P_N D^j S_tau(g)[e,...,e]||_(H^r)^2
          <= Vtilde_j ||e||inf^(2j)/M.

C and Vtilde_j depend on the fixed public parameters, not N or M.
Halting is almost sure. Dense storage is K plus one live finite tree.
The error target is the projected derivative; omitted Fourier bias is
not included in this inequality.

## Construction and proof interface

D44 supplies the actual field W=z h_(kappa a)(.-U), with tau/n<=a<=tau,
|z|<=n^j ||e||inf^j, mean equal to the actual derivative, hard j-query cap,
and preparation cost C(1+G)n. Its same heat estimate at index R gives

    E ||W||_(H^R)^2 <= V_(j,R) ||e||inf^(2j),
    V_(j,R)=H_R exp(lambda tau(B^(2j+R+d)-1)),
    H_R=(d+1)^(R-1)[b0^d+d(2R/theta)^R b1 b0^(d-1)],
    theta=4 pi^2 kappa tau,
    b0=1+sqrt(pi/theta), b1=1+sqrt(2pi/theta).

Only a fixed polynomial moment of n is used. No exponential population
moment or extra smoothness of e is assumed.

Independently draw a lattice vector with product distribution

    q1(0)=1/2,
    q1(k)=1/[2(|k|+1)(|k|+2)] for k!=0,
    q(nu)=product_l q1(nu_l).

Each coordinate is generated by floor(1/U0)-1 and an independent fair
sign, with a null endpoint guard. This takes a fixed number of ideal
operations even though the sampled coordinate has infinite absolute
first moment. Its finite realized value is not promised to fit a fixed
machine word. Telescoping gives total probability one and

    1/q(nu) <= 6^d (1+|nu|^2)^d.

One trial is zero if |nu|inf>N. Otherwise return the real mode

    Re[W_hat(nu) exp(2pi i nu.x)]/q(nu).

For nonzero nu choose the pair representative nu_star whose first
nonzero coordinate is positive. Its cosine and sine coefficients are
z exp(-2pi^2 kappa a|nu|^2) times, respectively, cos(2pi nu_star.U)/q(nu)
and sin(2pi nu_star.U)/q(nu). There is no factor two in an individual
trial: draws of both signs together provide that factor in expectation.
The zero coefficient is z/q(0). Actual correlations of z,a,U remain.

The conditional H^r second moment is at most 6^d ||W||_(H^R)^2.
Strong measurability and this bound give a genuine Bochner expectation,
including for the untruncated random single-mode field. Hence
Vtilde_j=6^d V_(j,R) works uniformly in N. The usual independent Hilbert
averaging identity proves the stated risk bound.

Draw nu before constructing the tree. An outside-box zero still counts
as a trial; it is not redrawn or conditioned away. Inside the box use
the actual D44 sampler. Its possible n<j zero also remains. Initialize
the dense K-array once, update at most two entries per trial, then scale
and serialize once. A base-(2N+1) fixed-dimensional index assigns the
cosine slot to nu_star, the sine slot to -nu_star and the constant to
zero. This proves the counted cost without a coefficient oracle or an
unpaid search over K entries at every sample.

## What the improvement does and does not say

The dense-per-trial derivative subroutine cost C M(1+G+K) is replaced
by C[M(1+G)+K]. The variance constant changes and can be much worse.
For an explicit comparison, take f=0, g=0, v=1, j=1 and N=0 in the
valid derivative domain. The dense constant sample is z>0. The sparse
sample equals 2^d z with probability 2^(-d), and zero otherwise, so its
variance exceeds the dense variance by (2^d-1) E z^2>0. No variance
dominance or finite-parameter speedup follows.

This interface can replace a derivative-averaging component with new
fixed constants. It does not remove the known-profile continuation
cost, prove exact Theta total work, improve the accepted exponential
rate, or establish a full fractional long-time theorem. No code,
numerics or new Lean formalization has been executed for D48. The
T89 Hilbert module supplies a general averaging bridge; it does not
encode this random lattice law or the Fourier/PDE correspondence.

Importance weighting and random Fourier sampling are classical. R23
records the exact bounded primary-source search, including failed PDF
access and the limited inspected kernel-feature comparison. No global
priority or significance conclusion is claimed.
