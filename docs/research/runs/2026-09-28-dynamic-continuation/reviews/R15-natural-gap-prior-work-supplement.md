## Primary sources inspected and exact limits

**Heinrich, Complexity of Initial Value Problems in Banach Spaces (2013).**
The journal PDF's abstract, introduction, problem/oracle definitions and
opening algorithm derivation were read, printed pages 73–80. Its input
is a pair (f,u0); an information functional returns an X-valued f(t,x)
or the whole X-valued initial datum u0. This differs substantially from
our scalar point-value access to an unknown spatial initial profile with
a fixed unbounded parabolic generator. Its randomized error definition
in the inspected section is expected norm, and its displayed algorithms
have fixed cardinality. The definitions do not directly yield our
growing-horizon, fixed-RMS, expected-query theorem. The remaining proof
and sharp theorem constants were not fully inspected. Source:
[journal PDF](https://nasplib.isofts.kiev.ua/bitstreams/460e8cd6-74c5-4822-8c8d-1fff2e0b7fe4/download).

**Fiedler and Rocha, Design of Sturm global attractors 2 (2024).**
The publisher abstract/introduction and Section 2's Chafee--Infante
setting were inspected. The paper studies qualitative connection graphs
for one-dimensional scalar parabolic equations with Neumann boundaries;
Section 2 relates the cubic reaction parameter to successive spectral
intervals and equilibrium counts. This confirms that attractor and
unstable-mode structure are established mathematical subjects, not new
discoveries of this project. Its cited scope is not our d-torus point
query model; the inspected portions provide no matching long-time
randomized information/work rate. The rest of its classification proof
was not read. Source:
[publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40863-023-00385-5).

**Kuehn, Piazzola and Ullmann, Uncertainty quantification analysis of
bifurcations of the Allen--Cahn equation with random coefficients (2024).**
The arXiv v2 abstract, introduction and opening deterministic bifurcation
analysis were read. The uncertain object is a reaction coefficient,
and the targets are random bifurcation points and branches. The paper
uses homogeneous Dirichlet boundaries and discusses simple-eigenvalue
conditions and the difficulty of higher-dimensional multiplicities.
These are useful neighboring questions for future multiple-unstable-mode
work, but changing the uncertain object and target would change our
research problem. The inspected portions do not establish our initial
point-query lower bound. No claim about the uninspected full proofs is
made. Source:
[arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.04639v2).

## Research implication, explicitly an inference

The useful distinction to investigate is quantitative randomized
information/work complexity as the horizon grows near an unstable
separator. Neither constructing a local stable graph nor identifying
spectral regimes is by itself an originality claim. These six queries
did not locate an exact matching theorem for our model; that bounded
absence is insufficient evidence of novelty. Full citation-chain and
theorem comparison remains open, as do significance and practical value.
