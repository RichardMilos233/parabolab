Substantive boundaries retained in every claim: the PDE and stochastic analytic bridges are conventional mathematics; the Lean module covers named deterministic algebra only. The error norm averages over space and the RMS averages over the ensemble of complete learned runs. Neither three realized runs nor an empirical variance establishes this population theorem. Floating-point arithmetic and special-function evaluation are not formally bounded. The raw obstruction concerns the specified λ and proposal, not every possible sampling law.
